# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Name

Manamoy Banerjee Professional Portfolio Website

Version: 2.0

Status: Planning & Design Phase

---

# 1. Problem Statement

Most student portfolios look generic and fail to communicate real technical capability, project depth, and career potential.

Recruiters often spend less than 30 seconds reviewing a candidate's profile. Traditional resumes provide limited insight into projects, technical skills, certifications, and personal growth.

The problem is that there is no single platform that effectively showcases Manamoy Banerjee's technical expertise, AI and Data Analytics skills, projects, certifications, achievements, learning journey, and professional identity in a compelling and recruiter-friendly format.

This portfolio website solves that problem by acting as a personal brand platform, technical showcase, and professional profile hub.
